# LIVED.

> A simple, thoughtful way to see your life in numbers.

**Live Demo:** [View LIVED.](https://lived-life-passed.vercel.app/)

---

## About

**LIVED.** is a minimalist life calculator that turns your date of birth into meaningful numbers.

Enter your date of birth and discover how many:

- Days you've lived
- Years you've lived
- Hours you've lived
- Minutes you've lived
- Seconds you've lived
- Approximate heartbeats you've experienced

You can also use **Remember Someone** mode to calculate the life span of someone who has passed away.

The idea is simple:

> **Every second counts.**

---

## Features

### ⏳ Life Calculator

Enter your date of birth and instantly see how long you've been alive.

### ⚡ Live Counters

Your seconds, minutes, hours and other time-based values continue updating while you stay on the page.

### ❤️ Estimated Heartbeats

See an approximate number of heartbeats based on the amount of time you've lived.

> Heartbeat values are estimates for informational purposes and are not medical measurements.

### 📅 Life in Weeks

Visualize your life as weeks to get a different perspective on the amount of time you've already lived.

### 🎂 Next Birthday

See how much time remains until your next birthday.

### 🕊️ Remember Someone

Enter both a birth date and a death date to calculate how long someone lived.

### 📸 Share Card

Generate a beautiful, shareable life card containing your calculated numbers.
